# Verification and proof scope

The main proof gives the upper-budget classification and its
oracle lower bound. The companion manuscripts establish fixed-dimensional,
single-matroid, support-geometric and approximation refinements. The source
map identifies their exact models and dependencies.

T is the sum of numeric targets, Q counts distinct nonempty support patterns,
d is fixed dimension where stated, and H charges encoded input and paid
primitive work. Zero-target preprocessing, mandatory original bases and exact
budgets remain part of the relevant theorem.

The matching-order lower bound is worst-case over parameter upper budgets,
not a claim that every exact pair is equally hard. Rank-query bounds,
deterministic-ETH consequences for readable programs, and statements about
specific represented classes are distinct. No executable algorithm or
proof-assistant formalization of these oracle theorems is included.

Read the [main proof](../proof.md) with its [source map](../sources.md). Repository checks validate links, mathematical syntax and catalogue coverage; they do not certify the asymptotic theorem. No proof-assistant verification or external peer review is claimed.
