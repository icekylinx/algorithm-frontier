\begin{abstract}
We give a deterministic reference implementation for maximum matching in
general graphs, with independently verifiable optimality certificates and
original input-edge identifiers. A cover-based reduction preserves every
matchable cover subset and reduces each blossom solve to at most three times
the component matching number. Including certificate construction, the
running time is \(O(n+m\log(m+2)+\sum_i\nu(G_i)^3)\), with linear
record storage. If a retained component is bipartite, both its crossing
compression and final solve have matching-number exponent \(5/2\), and
its certificate is constructed in linear additional work.
The certificate verifier performs no matching search. This paper proves the
cover compression and the simultaneous failed-forest certificate extraction,
proves the nonrecursive bipartite phase algorithm and its matching-number
phase bound, and states the boundary to the classical blossom algorithm
used for the other components. A separate layered construction gives one
fixed simple-graph kernel for every mixture of at most \(f\) native vertex
or edge failures, with at most \((f+3)\nu(G)\) vertices. Its universal
certificate uses only equally sized matching and vertex-cover witnesses.
\end{abstract}
