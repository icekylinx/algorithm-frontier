2. COEFFICIENT SPANS AND UNIT RANK OVER ARTIN RINGS

The ambient rings are finite tensor products of monomial local quotients,
with the surviving monomials as the field-vector-space basis. The
coefficient-span lemma is correctly restricted to scalar multiplication
and compatible matrix multiplication ON THE LEFT of a row family. Even
when a multiplier uses future variables, convolution creates only field
linear combinations of existing coefficient rows. Monomial truncation
deletes terms; it does not identify a higher monomial with a lower one.
A left matrix whose constant term is invertible has an inverse in the
finite local ring, giving equality in both directions.

Unit rank s means constant rank s plus vanishing of all (s+1)-minors in
the quotient. It does not mean a vaguely defined generic rank in a ring
with zero divisors. A constant s-pivot is a unit. The Schur-complement
identity follows by division by that unit determinant, which is legal.
Consequently an interval of unit rank s with a unit active s-pivot has a
row graph and any row in its row module with zero active coordinates is
zero. Constant invertible cut gauges preserve this property by
Cauchy--Binet. These facts are sufficient for every reset in the proof.

The rank-zero and full-rank cases agree with the definition: rank zero
means the matrix itself vanishes, and at full width the larger-minor
condition is empty. Trivial variable blocks are allowed and have support
cost zero. These edge cases are used correctly later for empty chunks
and constant endpoint factors.

3. THE WEIGHTED STABLE-PHASE INDUCTION

I independently reconstructed the source/reset identities, rather than
assuming the conclusion from the corresponding field argument.

On a stable active phase, let H be a field basis of the fixed active
coefficient space and J a right inverse. The constant witnesses B_i are
propagated through the constant matrices. In the block decomposition
  A_i=[E, EC; DE, DEC+F],
the active coordinate matrix R_i has identity constant term and is a
unit. Normalizing B_(i-1) A_i by R_i gives
  W_i=[H, B_(i),passive + Z_i],
where Z_i has zero constant term and depends only on the current block.
The corrected passive transition Ftilde_i is obtained by subtracting the
active lift. For a passive coefficient row p in K_(i-1), the identities
  (0,p)A_i-(pDEJ)W_i=(0,p Ftilde_i)
and
  K_i=Coeff(Z_i)+Coeff(K_(i-1) Ftilde_i)
are valid in the monomial ring. They use only unit inversions and field
linear coefficient-space identities.

The evolution formulas for an arbitrary controlled reachable row are
  a_i=a_(i-1)R_i+p_(i-1)DEJ,
  p_i=p_(i-1)Ftilde_i+a_i Z_i.
These include controls using future blocks. Such a controlled row need
not belong to the prefix polynomial subalgebra. What the proof needs,
and what remains true, is that its coefficient rows belong to the actual
prefix coefficient space. The left-multiplication lemma supplies exactly
that assertion. Field right inverses extract its active coordinates;
subtracting the corresponding B_i lift keeps coefficient reachability
and leaves a row in K_i.

On an interval whose passive constant product is zero, the full interval
has a unit active pivot and the exact graph identity. An initial active
control cancels the final active output of any chosen initial passive row.
The graph then forces its entire final output to vanish. Expanding the
passive evolution yields the RESET identity, expressing the initial-row
contribution as a sum of later Z_j sources times passive suffix products.
The final source is absent because the final active control is zero.
No cancellation by a nonunit, fraction-field rank, or lift of a quotient
identity to the polynomial ring is used.

The RESET identity is used for UNWEIGHTED coefficient-span inclusion.
The potentially complicated controls are not assigned small supports.
Only after removing them by the coefficient-span inclusion does the proof
assign one current-block support cost to the genuine local Z_j source.
This separation is the central point preventing future-control dependence
from invalidating the quantitative induction.

For a source at cut j, the restart uses the unchanged actual suffix word
and the coefficient-reachable initial space spanned by B_j and the source
row. Its active output space stays inside the global stable space, while
the constant B_j witnesses supply all of that space. Thus it has exactly
the same stable active space, not a newly assumed rank profile. The same
normalization/source formulas are available. The proof never assumes
rank constraints for the auxiliary matrices Ftilde_i.

The recursion is well founded. First establish the positive-passive-

5. BOX QUOTIENTS: EVERY LEADING MONOMIAL

For a chosen monomial X^alpha of the first nonzero ordinary homogeneous
scalar component, set variables outside its support to zero and quotient
by X_i^(alpha_i+1). Every lower-total-degree scalar coefficient is zero;
every other monomial of degree at least |alpha| either is alpha or dies.
Thus the surviving scalar is exactly its nonzero coefficient times
X^alpha. Original interval rank identities pass to the quotient and the
preserved constant pivots remain units. The Artin row-span bound must
therefore witness this unique monomial, bounding its support.

This proves a bound on EVERY monomial of the first component, not merely

6. LOCAL HASSE PIVOTS WITH CORRELATED SYMBOLIC SHIFTS

For an original-field rational vector F(T) with at most q entries, take
generic greedy Hasse row pivots over the original rational function field.
If mu is a nonpivot, a relation expressing D^[mu]F in smaller jets can be
Hasse differentiated by nu-mu. Every jet on the right has order below nu;
the left is binom(nu,mu)D^[nu]F. Hence any digit suborder of a pivot is
also a pivot. In characteristic p this gives
  product_e(nu_e+1)<=q and wt_p(nu)<=q-1;
in characteristic zero it gives nu<=q-1.

Substitution T=C preserves all required nonzero minors and rational
relations provided C is transcendental over the ORIGINAL coefficient
field. C need not be transcendental over the whole current coefficient
field, which contains C. The latter would be impossible and is not used.
A functional over that larger field, even one depending on C and all old
seeds, has its first nonzero contracted jet among the same pivots.

For any chosen first homogeneous word monomial, fixing all other local
exponents makes each remaining exponent the first nonzero local contracted
jet: a smaller exponent would give a nonzero lower-total-degree scalar
coefficient. Applying the rational-vector lemma to all entries of one
W-by-W local matrix yields exponent digit weight at most W^2-1.
This first-order-filtration argument is stronger than a coefficient-span
replacement claim and remains valid with arbitrarily correlated shifts.

Every current latent shift is c_i+ell_i(t,t^2,...,t^n) plus terms in fresh
amplitudes/directions independent of t. Since ell_i is nonzero, its t-part

7. AFFINE TRANSPORT AND THE FILTERED CHUNK INTERFACE

In positive characteristic a monomial is a product of its digit copies.
An invertible linear substitution expands each p-power copy by Frobenius,
so the digit degree cannot increase. The transformed first ordinary
homogeneous component is still nonzero because the linear map is
invertible. Ordinary homogeneity prevents higher components from cancelling
it after a homogeneous fresh SV substitution.

A polynomial of digit degree at most k has ordinary variable support at
most k in every monomial. SV_k contains every coordinate k-plane by
specializing its directions to the Lagrange nodes. Hence it hits the
nonzero transformed first component. This is applied to the whole first
component established above, not to an isolated latent-coordinate witness
that could disappear after linear transport.

For a test interval, fix outside exponents of any chosen leading monomial
before examining a prepared chunk. The remaining expression is a scalar
contraction of the chunk with endpoints in the old-seed coefficient field.
All smaller chunk total degrees vanish, since the other exponents are
fixed and the original monomial is of globally first degree. Therefore
the chunk part is a monomial of that contraction's first component. The
Artin support bound applies with precisely those field-constant endpoints.
There is no conditioning on fresh polynomial variables in an endpoint.
