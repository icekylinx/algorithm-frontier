1. GENERAL QUANTIFIED SCOPE: PASS

The public statements retain one unknown ROABP order, an unknown
invertible affine coordinate change, original-field local matrices,
arbitrary local generic ranks, arbitrary local entry degrees, and a
bound D on scalar total degree. They exclude singular affine projections
and orbit closures. Neither the hidden representation nor its order is
treated as an input to the construction.

The stated generator is the ordinary moment curve plus K(w) pairs,
with the same rank-stage constants as the frozen core. The pure
SV_{K(w)+1} claim is preserved. The public complexity is explicitly XP
in width, rather than an FPT claim. The finite-grid minimum is described
as a global choice between two complete arguments. Extension-field
oracle evaluations and the working-field size are explicit.

The public description does not pretend that the local representation
can be truncated to scalar total degree in the arbitrary-rank core.
The grid interpolation is applied to the original scalar, not to
auxiliary minors of possibly larger degree.

2. WEIGHTED RESET AND LEADING-COMPONENT TRANSPORT: PASS

The proof companion correctly distinguishes the unweighted reset

The rank descent retains actual contiguous block, adjacent-block and
tail tests. It does not omit the tail condition or silently replace the
target interval by an unrelated word.

4. STRUCTURAL SHARPNESS VERSUS PIT LOWER BOUNDS: PASS

The palette manuscript states exactly which upper theorem is imported
from the core and supplies its own lower construction and active-
endpoint argument. The physical-interval rank claim is distinguished
from endpoint augmentation. In particular, the lifted full augmented
scalar interval is acknowledged to drop from rank one to rank zero at
the moment curve for nonconstant examples.

The actual-curve lift uses two univariate physical factors per edge;
it does not treat a multivariate macro as a legal local factor. Its
interval explanation accounts for partial boundary macros without
commuting a shear through a projection.

The examples are expressly said to survive one SV pair, with and
without the initial curve. The manuscript and public summaries deny
the unsupported inference to seed-count, hitting-set-size or query
lower bounds. They also decline to identify every extremal abstract
palette word with an exterior power of a smaller word. These are the
correct boundaries for the structural result.
