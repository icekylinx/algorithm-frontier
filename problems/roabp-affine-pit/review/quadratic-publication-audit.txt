1. EXACT DOMAIN AND CONCLUSION

The theorem assumes w>=2, N=w^2-1, and prime characteristic
  p>N+w-1=w^2+w-2.
It constructs an original-field width-w read-once word in n=p observed
coordinates, with three active variables and identity padding. Every
physical local factor is generically invertible and invertible at
gamma_i=t^i. The scalar has degree exactly 2p(w-1), vanishes on gamma,
and has whole first nonzero residual component (-Y_p)^N.

Since N<p, that component has characteristic digit degree N. The theorem
does not claim the example in characteristic zero, in the excluded small
characteristics, or under a derivative-separating curve. The dependence
n=p and the use of only three active coordinates are consistent.

2. BINOMIAL DETERMINANT: SIGN AND NONVANISHING MODULO p

For Lemma 2, factoring
  c_j=N!/((k_j+w-1)!(N-k_j)!)
from column j leaves
  P_i(k)=product_{a=i+1}^{w-1}(k+a)
         product_{b=0}^{i-1}(N-k-b).
The stated bounds on k_j ensure all factorial arguments are nonnegative,
and direct cancellation recovers binom(N,k_j+i).

At q_s=N-s, the entry P_i(q_s) vanishes for i>s. The diagonal is
  P_s(q_s)=s! (N+w-1-s)!/N!.
The Vandermonde on the q_s is
  (-1)^(w(w-1)/2) product_{s=0}^{w-1}s!.
Dividing the triangular evaluation determinant by this Vandermonde gives
exactly the coefficient-basis determinant and the sign written in (1).
Restoring all column factors gives the displayed consecutive-binomial
determinant formula, with no missing sign or factorial factor.

For k_j=wj and N=w^2-1, the largest k_j is N-w+1. Every factorial in
the formula has argument at most N+w-1<p, so every factorial denominator
and numerator is a unit modulo p. Every nonzero Vandermonde difference
w(b-a) lies strictly between zero and p. The determinant is therefore
nonzero in the field. This explicitly justifies modular nonvanishing;
nonvanishing over the rationals alone would not suffice.

3. MIDDLE MATRIX AND ITS DETERMINANT

The map (i,j) -> i+wj is a bijection from 0<=i,j<w to 0,...,N.
Hence every exponent N-i-wj of T is nonnegative, and every coefficient
binom(N,i+wj) is nonzero because p>N.

The row u(C)=(1,C,...,C^(w-1)) and column
v(C)=(1,C^w,...,C^(w(w-1))) give
  u(C)A(T)v(C)
   =sum_{m=0}^N (-1)^(N-m) binom(N,m) C^m T^(N-m)
   =(C-T)^N.
There is no index collision or omitted term.

For any determinant permutation, both the total T exponent and the total
sign exponent from the entries are
  E=wN-sum_i i-w sum_i sigma(i)=w(w^2-1)/2.
This is an integer. The permutation sign is still supplied by the
binomial determinant, so
  det A(T)=(-1)^E T^E det[binom(N,i+wj)]
has exactly the stated sign. It is a nonzero scalar multiple of T^E.
It is not a polynomial unit at every value of T, and the manuscript does
not claim that. It is nonzero generically and becomes a unit in F(t)
at T=t^p, which is the property needed for physical interval ranks.

The entries are distinct nonzero scalar multiples of 1,T,...,T^N, so
their original-field scalar span has dimension N+1=w^2. After ordering
and scaling those coordinates, the Hasse jet matrix has zeros when
degree<order and diagonal entries one. Its leading square submatrices
are invertible. The claimed generic pivots 0,...,N and last-order
contraction are therefore correct.

4. ORIGINAL-FIELD REALIZATION AND EXACT SCALAR DEGREE

U(X) changes only the first row of the identity, preserving its first
entry as one; V(Z) does the analogous operation on the first column.
Their nonidentity parts square to zero and have zero diagonal. They are
triangular unipotents, so det U=det V=1 in every characteristic.

U, A, and V are univariate polynomial matrices over the original field.
The shift parameter t occurs nowhere in their coefficients. With constant
endpoints, their scalar has the term indexed by (i,j)
  (-1)^(N-i-wj) binom(N,i+wj)
  X^(ip) T^(N-i-wj) Z^(jp).
Distinct pairs have distinct X,Z exponents, so no such terms cancel.

Its total degree is N+i(p-1)+j(p-w). Both slopes are positive under
the theorem's threshold. The unique maximum is i=j=w-1; its T exponent
is zero and its coefficient is one. Its degree is exactly 2p(w-1).
Thus the nonzero scalar and exact degree claims are correct, rather
than merely upper bounds.

5. GENUINE MOMENT-CURVE SHIFT AND EVERY PHYSICAL INTERVAL

The active variables are x_1, x_p, x_w, which are distinct because
2<=w<p. At gamma, with C=t^p, their p-th-power values produce exactly
u(C), middle argument C, and v(C). Equation (5) gives f(gamma)=0.
The remaining observed variables are represented by identity factors.
This is a legitimate read-once order of n=p independent variables.

Every generic local determinant is nonzero. At the curve the outer
determinants remain one and det A(C) is nonzero in F(t). Determinants
multiply, so every contiguous physical interval has rank w both
generically and at gamma, regardless of identity padding. No assertion
about scalar-endpoint-augmented intervals follows from this, and the
source explicitly excludes that stronger assertion.

Every t^i, including t^p, is transcendental over the original field.
The arguments are correlated, but they are genuine substitutions of
an original-field word into the actual prescribed moment curve.

6. THE WHOLE FIRST RESIDUAL COMPONENT

By Frobenius,
  (t+Y_1)^p=C+Y_1^p,
  (t^w+Y_w)^p=C^w+Y_w^p.
All nonconstant residual terms of U and V have ordinary degree at
least p. Middle entries are polynomials, so multiplication cannot lower
that degree. Since p>N, no endpoint perturbation contributes to any
component of degree at most N.

Those components come only from
  u(C)A(C+Y_p)v(C)=(-Y_p)^N.
Thus all smaller components vanish and the ENTIRE degree-N component
is the stated one-variable monomial. No unfiltered coefficient-span
inference is being used. Because 0<N<p, its digit weight is N.

7. SV1 COEFFICIENT AND SCOPE

Under Y_i=z lambda_i(y), each ordinary residual component of degree d
has amplitude degree d. Hence the coefficient of z^N is exactly
  (-lambda_p(y))^N,
which is nonzero. Higher residual degrees, including endpoint
perturbations beginning at degree p, cannot cancel it. This proves
symbolic nonvanishing under gamma+SV1, as asserted.

The source correctly separates individual transcendence from derivative
separation: gamma_p'=0. It also correctly notes that the local entry
degree delta is at least (w-1)p, so a hypothesis p>delta is not satisfied.
The example therefore does not refute those stronger-hypothesis results.

Any universal width-only upper bound for the stated individually
transcendental class must be at least w^2-1, because the construction
exists for every qualifying characteristic. This lower bound on the
whole-first-component quantity does not imply a generator or query lower
bound, and the explicit SV1 coefficient demonstrates that distinction.
