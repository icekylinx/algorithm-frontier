# Scope audit

- `B`, `B0`, `Delta`, and `p` are local model parameters; they are not interchangeable with binary input length.
- T's common-order and bounded-family theorems do not extend to arbitrary row-dependent weights; the boundary is recorded separately.
- U's sparse theorem requires positive root charge for every quota-active coordinate and retains the original completion oracle.
- Ground kernels reduce the explicit selectable ground while preserving access to the original resource and profit interfaces; they are not full-input bit kernels.
