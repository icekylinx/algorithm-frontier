## Interpretation conventions

- All new algorithms stated as deterministic have deterministic guarantees; public randomized comparisons remain labeled randomized.
- Ordinary ground kernels preserve original item identities with access to the original oracles. The positive-capacity kernel retains the original zero ground and completion oracle. Neither notion compresses every oracle-program bit.
- The original required resource total is retained after masks and capacity restrictions. Optional problems receive a zero-cost disposal coordinate; mandatory problems do not.
- Integer coefficients, target magnitudes, and binary counts are charged in their stated models. No binary-large resource expansion is concealed in a polynomial preprocessing claim.
- Signed scalar optimization is exact. Several unrelated scalar budgets or arbitrary additional oracle resources are not automatically covered.
- Complete internal proof review is not formal verification or external peer review. The source comparisons are bounded checks, not proofs of literature absence or publication priority.
