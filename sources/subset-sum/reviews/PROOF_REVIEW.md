## Current results and scope

Part I proves time $C+\operatorname{poly}(n,L)2^{En}$, with
$E=149809813343531/301754962128739<0.4964618056$. Part II proves
time $\operatorname{poly}(n,L)2^{n/2}$ and writable-bit space
$\operatorname{poly}(n,L)2^{E_Sn}$, where
$E_S=18261635770550353/10^{17}<0.1826163578$; the displayed decimal bounds for both parts use ten places.
These are separate algorithms. Both cover arbitrary signed binary
inputs and exact decisions with indexed witness recovery.
