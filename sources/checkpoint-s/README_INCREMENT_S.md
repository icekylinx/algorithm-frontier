## Input conventions

All algorithms are deterministic. Their resource is one integral polymatroid or its ordinary matroid special case. The numeric targets and positive coefficients are integers, while the separately optimized scalar may be rational and signed. Resource ranks and counts are binary encoded. Original mandatory totals are preserved.

The query lower bounds are unconditional in the general rank-oracle model and allow bounded-error randomized decision. Computational lower bounds for readable rank programs/circuits use deterministic ETH; their randomized variants require randomized ETH separately.

No result here is a lower bound for ordinary graph matching or every explicitly represented special matroid class.
