## Incorporated technical refinements

- Weak cut-or-expander success retains a <code class="math">1-\beta</code> fraction of its current input, with <code class="math">\beta=1/1000</code>; strong-wrapper coverage and exact root coverage are accounted for separately.
- Continuing abstract games use cut sparsity at most <code class="math">1/8</code>, and repeated matching occurrences retain distinct edge identifiers.
- The virtual root has one physical embedding. Child paths enter it by literal graph inclusion before that map is applied.
- Both witness-routing directions pay their full host-size-times-load query volume.
- Hypercube weighted witnesses carry their explicit polynomial-logarithmic degree and inventory multipliers.
- Individual ambient images and token paths are normalized at their respective immediate lifetime identities.
- Corrections use the current route, with central native identifiers recorded separately from geometric occurrences.
- Initial child-update credit can be spent late and is charged at child update cost.
- The final inverse-quality bound includes every recursive level.
- Original-edge tree export gives additive stored-label bit growth. Weighted forest counts include the length aspect, and larger temporary arithmetic remains construction work.
- A physical lifetime's inactive port fiber remains counted until its period ends; retirement preserves both signed and absolute motion.
